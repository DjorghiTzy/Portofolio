01 — About

Built in the field, not in a spreadsheet

How 18 months in retail and logistics shaped the way I work.

I started out in retail and logistics — one year and five months as a Delivery Man at Indomaret, plus a month on the shop floor as a Pramuniaga. That work taught me things a book cannot: how to read a customer, how to stay calm when the route is packed, and how to keep a promise about time.

Every day meant targets, tight schedules and dozens of face-to-face conversations. That built the habits I lean on now: discipline, careful record-keeping, and being friendly without being asked. I'm pointing those same habits at bigger sales and customer-service work next.

  • 01 On time A delivery window is a promise, not an estimate.
  • 02 Friendly first People remember how you treated them long after they forget the price.
  • 03 Own the problem Wrong address, empty stock, heavy rain — find the way through anyway.
